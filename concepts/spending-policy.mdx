# Spending policy

Before the Hub signs spend, it evaluates your agent's policy (Postgres, plus on-chain Soroban checks when configured for that network).

## Checks

| Control | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Per-transaction cap | Max USDC-equivalent per spend |
| Daily cap | Rolling daily USDC-equivalent total |
| Allow / deny lists | Destination address rules |
| Pause | Blocks spend until you unpause |

Amounts are evaluated in USDC terms (including XLM transfers via live FX). DEX swaps do not count toward daily outbound spend caps the same way transfers do. See [Swap](/guides/swap).

## Who can change policy

**Dashboard only.** Agents can call `get_policy_status` and `spending_report`. They cannot mutate caps or lists via MCP.

Configure limits in the Hub: [Set policy limits](/guides/policy-limits).
